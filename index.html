GLASSWAVE

By Adenola • Morning Walk • A glassy, purple Video.js v10 skin. Shot in a real Nigerian estate — quiet street, green lawns, houses.

Design thinking: Glasswave turns Video.js v10 into a soft, glassy purple stage for real estate stories. I used my own estate walk video so controls are tested on real bright outdoor footage. Controls stay readable with dark gradient + white text, and 48px touch targets make it easy on phones.
